# Echo Debt — Finalist Development

## Core promise
Faster-than-light messages can arrive from histories that once existed but no longer do.

## World bible
Human expansion depends on relay infrastructure that moves information faster than ships. Rare relay faults produce cryptographically valid packets that have no origin in current history.

Researchers call them echoes. Public systems discard them as corruption. Specialists know they can contain coherent records, warnings and personal messages.

An echo is not a window into a stable parallel universe. Receiving information changes the receiver's decisions and therefore which future histories remain reachable. Information can survive the loss of the history that created it; matter cannot cross.

## Film #1 — 18 minute beat sheet
**0:00–1:30 — Her own distress call.** Recovery engineer Mara Venn approaches an abandoned relay. Her receiver authenticates a message in her own voice: "Do not open compartment four."

**1:30–4:00 — Already open.** Mara and pilot Ivo dock. Compartment four stands open although station logs say it is sealed.

**4:00–6:30 — Contradictory evidence.** The relay contains valid logs describing two incompatible versions of the same accident.

**6:30–9:00 — Ivo dies.** A new packet shows Ivo dying during a decompression event minutes from now. They avoid the predicted cause.

**9:00–11:30 — Wrong lesson.** Avoiding it triggers details from a second packet. Mara realizes the messages are not predictions.

**11:30–14:00 — Lost histories.** They decode packets from multiple mutually exclusive outcomes. In one, Mara died. In another, Ivo did. Each sender believed theirs was the only history.

**14:00–16:30 — The choice.** A relay cascade begins. Physical evidence says one shutdown sequence is correct; an echo from a now-impossible Mara says the opposite. Mara chooses the echo.

**16:30–17:30 — Survival.** The relay is destroyed, but they escape. Mara finally accepts evidence can be true without belonging to her reality.

**17:30–18:00 — Aperture.** A final packet arrives from "Earth," authenticated by human infrastructure, but its stellar coordinates identify a different home system.

## Character arc
Mara moves from institutional certainty toward accountable uncertainty: evidence matters, but context determines what truth means.

## Reveal budget
Film #1 answers:
- echoes are coherent information from mutually exclusive histories;
- they are not straightforward predictions;
- acting on them changes what remains reachable.

Film #1 does NOT answer:
- why relay technology accesses echoes;
- whether histories are destroyed or merely inaccessible;
- why another human Earth exists;
- whether someone can deliberately create/select echoes.

## Visual development
Industrial relay hardware, redundant displays disagreeing with one another, authenticated messages, subtle repeated compositions with changed details. Avoid multiverse clichés such as portals and duplicate armies.

Signature image: two adjacent monitors showing equally authenticated footage of incompatible deaths.

## GenAI / continuity risk
Medium. Two consistent leads and one complex station. Contradictory versions of shots require deliberate continuity control but also play directly to FilmForge's provenance system.

## Three-film test
1. **Echo Debt** — discovery that discarded histories can communicate.
2. **Witness War** — legal and political systems confront evidence from incompatible histories.
3. **No Return Address** — an expedition follows sustained echoes from humanity based around another star.

## Franchise health
Exceptional. The mechanism naturally generates new dilemmas at personal through civilizational scale.
