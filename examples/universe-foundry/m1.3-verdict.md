# M1.3 Semifinal Verdict

All five semifinalists can support a reference film. The stress test therefore ranks creative fit rather than merely pass/fail.

## Tier A — finalists

### Dead Channel
**Reference-film strength:** exceptional  
**Universe strength:** excellent

The simplest audience promise, lowest exposition burden and strongest contained 15–20 minute production. The final "We heard you" escalation opens the universe without explaining it.

Primary development question: how do we keep the phenomenon rigorously science-fictional and prevent it drifting into ghost-story logic?

### Echo Debt
**Reference-film strength:** excellent  
**Universe strength:** exceptional

The deepest reusable story engine. Alternate-history information creates moral problems at personal, legal, political and civilizational scale.

Primary development question: can the rules be understood through action and emotion without timeline exposition?

### Memory Reef
**Reference-film strength:** excellent  
**Universe strength:** excellent

The strongest visual brand and emotional core. A single image of a crystalline wreck containing human memories communicates much of the premise.

Primary development question: what exact mechanism limits reef memories so they cannot solve arbitrary mysteries?

## Tier B — reserve finalists

### Pale Transit
Excellent contained mystery and strong sequel hooks. It needs visual development that makes pale transit unmistakably its own place.

### Census Zero
The most unusual conceptual texture and perhaps the best low-budget premise. It needs more physical/cinematic behavior in Film #1.

## Recommendation

Advance **Dead Channel**, **Echo Debt**, and **Memory Reef** to finalist development.

Do not select the universe yet.

M1.4 should give each finalist:
1. a compact world bible,
2. a complete 15–20 minute beat sheet,
3. character arcs,
4. visual-development brief,
5. reveal budget (what Film #1 answers vs preserves),
6. continuity/generation risk analysis,
7. three-film expansion test.

Only then should FilmForge freeze its first canon.
