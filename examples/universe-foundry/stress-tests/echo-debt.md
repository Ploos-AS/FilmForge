# Echo Debt — Semifinal Stress Test

## Audience promise
Messages can arrive from lives that no longer happened — including your own.

## Protagonist
Mara Venn, a relay-recovery engineer who trusts recorded evidence more than people after a fatal mission inquiry destroyed her former crew.

**Want:** prove that facts can still be trusted.
**Wound:** she chose the official record over a crewmate's testimony and has never learned whether that choice was right.

## Opening image
A dead relay rotates above a gas giant. Mara hears her own voice on an emergency channel before her ship has transmitted anything.

## First two minutes
Mara and pilot Ivo dock to recover the relay's black box. The station clock is eleven minutes ahead. A distress packet arrives authenticated with Mara's key. Her recorded voice says: "Do not open compartment four."

Ivo points out that compartment four is already open.

## Midpoint discovery
The messages are not forecasts. They contain mutually exclusive histories, and each is internally authentic. One discarded history includes an Ivo who died saving Mara.

## Ending
Mara prevents the immediate disaster by trusting a message that contradicts the physical evidence. The relay is destroyed.

Back aboard their ship, the receiver prints one final packet addressed to Mara from a version of Earth whose coordinates do not match their Earth.

## Hard rules
1. Echoes carry information, never matter.
2. An echo can only originate from a history that once shared causal ancestry with the receiver.
3. Receiving information changes probability and may make the sender's history unreachable; there is no reliable conversation with one stable alternate timeline.

## Visual signature
Lived-in industrial space hardware, duplicated waveforms, subtly contradictory displays, delayed reflections and impossible authenticated recordings.

## Future films
- **The Witness War:** courts and governments confront evidence from mutually exclusive histories.
- **No Return Address:** explorers follow echoes from a human civilization absent from their universe.
- **The Mercy Protocol:** a colony learns that preventing its catastrophe may erase the only history able to warn others.

## Stress result
Very strong franchise engine. Main challenge is communicating the rules with emotional clarity rather than exposition.
