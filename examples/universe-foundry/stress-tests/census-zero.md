# Census Zero — Semifinal Stress Test

## Audience promise
Everyone remembers these people. Their records are perfect. The only problem is that they never existed.

## Protagonist
Eli Renn, an interplanetary census auditor who believes civilization survives because records outlast memory.

**Want:** reconcile a statistical anomaly before the annual census closes.
**Wound:** after a neurological illness damaged parts of his autobiographical memory, Eli relies on official records to tell him who he has been.

## Opening image
An apartment looks recently occupied: warm cup, unmade bed, messages waiting. Facial recognition says nobody has ever entered it.

## First two minutes
Eli investigates citizen Tomas Vey, whose taxes, employment, medical history and friendships span 34 years. Neighbors remember him. Photographs contain spaces where everyone insists Tomas stood.

The emergency-contact field lists Eli.

Eli has no memory of him.

## Midpoint discovery
There are millions of zero citizens. Their records interlock too deeply to be fabricated. Removing one causes legitimate histories of real people to stop making sense.

## Ending
Eli files Tomas as real despite the absence of physical evidence.

The census system accepts the correction, then updates Eli's own status.

Existence verification: ZERO.

His apartment door opens behind him. Someone says, "You finally noticed."

## Hard rules
1. Zero citizens have coherent social and administrative histories but lack direct physical traces.
2. Human memories of them are sincere and mutually consistent, not simple implanted scripts.
3. Altering their records changes institutional reality, not physical history — at least initially.

## Visual signature
Warm lived-in spaces with precise absences, severe civic systems, archival imagery that compositionally implies missing people, and bureaucratic interfaces treated as suspense objects.

## Future films
- **Dependent:** a child legally exists only because her zero-citizen parent does.
- **The Correction Office:** an institution quietly decides which contradictory people count as real.
- **Census One:** physical reality begins conforming to an administrative correction.

## Stress result
Most intellectually unusual finalist and highly producible. Needs strong visual direction and physical stakes to avoid becoming a dialogue-heavy procedural.
