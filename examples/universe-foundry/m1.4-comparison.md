## Interpretation

### Dead Channel
Best reference-production candidate. It lets FilmForge concentrate on directing, atmosphere, sound, editing and character consistency rather than immediately fighting the hardest possible visual-generation problem.

Its risk is conceptual drift: the channel must remain a measurable science-fiction phenomenon rather than becoming supernatural shorthand.

### Echo Debt
Best pure long-term story engine. It may ultimately support the broadest range of stories, but Film #1 must teach its rules elegantly.

It is an excellent candidate for a later FilmForge production even if it does not become the first universe.

### Memory Reef
Best visual/emotional brand. It is arguably the most poster-ready concept, but stable reef morphology, embedded memories and age variants create substantially more generation/compositing work.

It is valuable as a future test of FilmForge's more advanced continuity and VFX capabilities.

## M1.4 recommendation

Use **Dead Channel** as the leading candidate for FilmForge's first reference production.

Do not yet delete or merge the other universes. Preserve Echo Debt and Memory Reef as independent IP candidates.

Before canon freeze, M1.5 should run a production proof:
- 8–12 representative shot specifications;
- character reference brief for Sera;
- Kestrel station visual bible;
- receiver/console prop bible;
- sound identity;
- three key image prompts;
- opening 90-second animatic plan;
- final chorus sequence plan.

If Dead Channel survives that proof, freeze Film #1 canon and begin screenplay development.
