# M2.2 Prompt Package

These are provider-neutral starting prompts. Provider adapters may translate syntax, but must not change canon intent.

## Sera — identity candidate

Grounded cinematic character reference for Sera Vale, early-to-mid-30s communications technician at a remote deep-space listening station. Competent working professional, natural human features, short-to-medium dark hair with a simple reproducible silhouette, practical charcoal utility workwear over a muted undershirt, station identification patch on left chest, compact wrist terminal. Reserved observant neutral expression. Plain reference background, realistic anatomy, restrained near-future science fiction, tactile documentary realism. No glamour styling, armor, neon, cyberpunk fashion, elaborate hair or jewelry focus.

## Kestrel — listening room

Wide production-design reference of the Kestrel deep-space listening station control room. Small human-scale utilitarian communications facility, aging but carefully maintained, accumulated equipment from several upgrade generations, physical receiver wall behind the primary operator chair, viewport camera-left, main entry rear-right, practical service panels and cable trunks, motivated work lighting, dark space beyond the viewport. Restrained grounded near-future science fiction. No holograms, neon cyberpunk, luxury spaceship styling or arbitrary cathedral scale.

## Legacy receiver

Industrial communications receiver reference, older than surrounding station electronics but still professional equipment: heavy rack enclosure, physical speaker grille, tactile tuning controls, small spectral display, simple power indicator, removable audio lead. Designed for believable maintenance and repeated visual continuity. No antique wooden radio, steampunk, magical markings, supernatural glow or decorative sci-fi clutter.

## Kestrel exterior

Extreme-wide cinematic production-design reference: a tiny inhabited listening-station module attached to a vast functional antenna structure in deep space. Human scale should feel insignificant against communications infrastructure and darkness. Grounded engineering, restrained surface detail, no heroic battleship silhouette, no weapons, no colorful nebula spectacle.

## Look test

Quiet grounded science-fiction frame, 2.39:1 composition, stable geometry, negative space, practical motivated lighting, tactile maintained industrial surfaces, restrained natural contrast, human-scale technology, no supernatural visual language, no neon cyberpunk, no blockbuster lens flare, no excessive shallow depth of field.
