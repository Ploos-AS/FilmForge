# Final Chorus — Sequence Plan

## Objective
Escalate one intimate impossible conversation into evidence of a universe-scale phenomenon without explaining it.

## Sequence
1. Cael disappears into normal receiver noise.
2. Sera waits longer than is comfortable.
3. She begins her report.
4. A receiver at the far left of the wall physically clicks awake.
5. Another activates elsewhere.
6. Then another. The pattern spreads irregularly, not as a decorative wave.
7. Individual voices appear: different languages, ages and acoustic transmission qualities.
8. Sera stops trying to operate the console and simply listens.
9. Translation support begins finding semantic equivalence across voices.
10. The voices converge on the meaning: "We heard you."
11. Cut on Sera's realization, not on a VFX climax.
12. Exterior Kestrel. The station remains visually insignificant against space.

## Rules
- No ghosts, faces in screens or supernatural visual effects.
- No perfect choir synchronization.
- Do not reveal the source.
- Do not show a map filled with thousands of convenient dots.
- Sound expands the universe; imagery remains contained.

## Production proof
Build this sequence first as audio + storyboard/animatic. If it works without generated spectacle, the concept has passed its most important production test.
