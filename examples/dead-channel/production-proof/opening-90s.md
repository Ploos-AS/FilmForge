# Opening 90 Seconds — Animatic Plan

## 0:00–0:12
Exterior Kestrel. No music. Sparse station transmission and distant mechanical texture establish scale.

## 0:12–0:28
Locked listening-room wide. Sera crosses frame performing a familiar routine. Receiver wall is readable as geography, not exposition.

## 0:28–0:43
Detail sequence: cup, diagnostic checklist, receiver modules, unanswered personal-message indicator. Sera dismisses the message without opening it.

## 0:43–0:58
A sound comes from behind her. Not a sting — an ordinary human voice partially masked by equipment noise.

Sera stops.

## 0:58–1:12
She checks the receiver wall. One unit has no active power indicator. She disconnects its physical audio lead to confirm.

## 1:12–1:22
Sera turns away.

The disconnected speaker says: "Sera."

## 1:22–1:30
Hold on her. Cut to title.

## Proof requirement
The opening works only if atmosphere, spatial clarity and performance create the hook without lore narration or spectacular VFX.
