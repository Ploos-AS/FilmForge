# M1.5 Production Proof Verdict

## Decision: PASS

Dead Channel survives the production-proof gate.

The concept is unusually well matched to an AI-assisted reference production because its strongest cinematic tools are controllable:

- one primary on-screen character;
- one dominant interior location;
- one hero prop family;
- voice performance for the second lead;
- sound-driven escalation;
- UI that can be composited as deterministic assets;
- limited need for crowds, complex action or changing costumes;
- a finale whose scale is heard rather than rendered.

## Key production risks

### Character identity
Sera appears in many close and medium shots. Mitigation: lock a reference pack before shot generation and reject drift early.

### Station geography
The listening room recurs throughout the film. Mitigation: establish floor plan, reference renders and canonical camera angles.

### Generated text/UI
Do not rely on video models for readable interface text. Generate or composite UI separately.

### Performance
The film depends on listening and restrained reaction. Shot selection and voice timing matter more than visual spectacle.

### Sound
Sound is not post-production decoration here; it is part of the screenplay and should enter the pipeline during animatic development.

## Canon recommendation

Freeze **Dead Channel** as FilmForge Reference Universe #1 and begin screenplay development.

Preserve Echo Debt and Memory Reef as independent non-canon universe candidates for later productions.
