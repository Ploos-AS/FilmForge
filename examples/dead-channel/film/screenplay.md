# DEAD CHANNEL
## Screenplay — Draft 0.1

**Target runtime:** 18 minutes  
**Reference production:** FilmForge

---

## SC01 — KESTREL

**EXT. KESTREL LISTENING STATION — SPACE**

A small station clings to an antenna structure vastly larger than its inhabited module.

No music.

Sparse radio traffic. Equipment ticks.

**INT. KESTREL — LISTENING ROOM**

SERA VALE works through a diagnostic routine without urgency. One person maintaining too many receivers.

A personal-message indicator waits at the edge of her console.

She notices it.

Dismisses it unopened.

Sera checks receiver levels. Makes a note. Drinks cold coffee.

A voice, faint behind her.

**VOICE**
...Sera...

She freezes.

Looks toward the receiver wall.

Nothing unusual.

She crosses to an older receiver. Its power indicator is dark.

Sera pulls its audio lead.

Turns away.

**VOICE**
Sera.

She stops.

**TITLE: DEAD CHANNEL**

---

## SC02 — FORBIDDEN FREQUENCY

Sera reconnects the receiver through a diagnostic isolator.

A spectrum trace crawls across its small display.

She tunes.

Static.

Then ordinary life.

**DISTANT VOICE 1**
—put it by the door. I'll take it when—

Another voice laughs somewhere in the transmission.

Sera's expression changes before her hands do.

She checks a station reference.

A warning occupies the console:

**RESTRICTED SPECTRAL REGION — PASSIVE MONITORING ONLY**

She kills the audio.

Silence.

Sera waits.

Turns it back on.

A weather report. A local transit delay. Someone asking a child whether they ate.

Sera searches the archive.

A civilization record appears: same language family, same astronomical markers.

**STATUS: EXTINCT — 612 STANDARD YEARS**

Sera records a sample.

**SERA**
Kestrel incident log. Unscheduled reception inside restricted band. No transmission initiated.

She reaches to disable the receiver.

**CAEL (V.O.)**
Wait.

Her hand stops.

---

## SC03 — REPLY

Sera stares at the receiver.

**SERA**
Repeat.

Static.

**CAEL (V.O.)**
You said you were going to disable it.

Sera steps back.

**SERA**
Identify source.

**CAEL (V.O.)**
You first.

She looks at the disconnected path, then at the diagnostic routing.

**SERA**
This circuit is receive-only.

**CAEL (V.O.)**
So is mine.

A beat.

Sera moves to the main console and starts an isolated recording.

**SERA**
What did I say before you answered?

**CAEL (V.O.)**
"Kestrel incident log."

He repeats enough of her statement to prove he heard it.

Sera's professional calm narrows into concentration.

**SERA**
Name.

**CAEL (V.O.)**
Cael.

**SERA**
Location.

**CAEL (V.O.)**
No.

**SERA**
Then this ends.

**CAEL (V.O.)**
Sera Vale.

She says nothing.

**CAEL (V.O.)**
That's your name, isn't it?

---

## SC04 — CAEL

Sera routes every available diagnostic onto the signal.

Nothing produces a direction.

**SERA**
How did you get my name?

**CAEL (V.O.)**
You told me.

**SERA**
I didn't.

**CAEL (V.O.)**
Not today.

Sera looks toward the personal-message indicator. Still unopened.

**SERA**
What date is it?

Cael answers.

Sera checks it twice.

The year is wrong.

Not by years.

By centuries.

**CAEL (V.O.)**
Your turn.

Sera gives her date.

Cael laughs once. Not because it is funny.

**CAEL (V.O.)**
That's impossible.

**SERA**
Agreed.

**CAEL (V.O.)**
No. You don't understand.

A pause.

**CAEL (V.O.)**
There hasn't been anyone from Earth for four hundred and nine years.

Sera stops typing.

---

## SC05 — WRONG HISTORY

Sera asks for astronomical references.

Cael provides them reluctantly.

She compares pulsar timings. Orbital positions. Historical events.

Some align.

Others do not.

She brings up Cael's civilization.

The archive describes its final century.

**SERA**
Your world is listed here.

**CAEL (V.O.)**
Good.

**SERA**
No.

She hesitates.

**SERA**
It's listed as uninhabited.

Silence from Cael.

**CAEL (V.O.)**
Since when?

Sera does not answer immediately.

**CAEL (V.O.)**
Sera.

**SERA**
Six hundred and twelve years.

Cael breathes.

The most ordinary sound in the film.

**CAEL (V.O.)**
That's not possible.

**SERA**
I know.

**CAEL (V.O.)**
You keep saying that like it helps.

Sera almost responds sharply.

Doesn't.

On her console, an old incident summary catches her eye.

A phrase matches something Cael mentioned earlier.

She opens the record.

---

## SC06 — THE WARNING

Archived data: atmospheric interference. Emergency power restrictions. Civil communications failure.

Sera listens as Cael casually mentions lights flickering where he is.

She checks the archive again.

Same sequence.

**SERA**
Cael. What color is the sky?

**CAEL (V.O.)**
What?

**SERA**
Outside. Right now.

He looks, somewhere we never see.

**CAEL (V.O.)**
There's a band near the horizon. Green.

Sera closes her eyes.

The archive records an unusual green atmospheric band before the civilization's last confirmed transmissions.

A protocol warning appears as Sera touches transmit controls.

**ACTIVE TRANSMISSION PROHIBITED**

She withdraws her hand.

**CAEL (V.O.)**
What is it?

Sera looks at the unopened personal message.

**CAEL (V.O.)**
Sera?

She opens the message.

We do not hear its contents.

We only see her listen for a few seconds.

Then stop it.

She turns back to the receiver.

**SERA**
You need to leave.

**CAEL (V.O.)**
Leave what?

**SERA**
Wherever you are. Get below ground if you can. Away from the grid. Take anyone you can reach.

**CAEL (V.O.)**
Why?

The protocol warning pulses.

**SERA**
Because my records say your world is about to die.

---

## SC07 — TRANSMIT

The channel erupts in interference.

**CAEL (V.O.)**
How long?

Sera searches the archive.

There is no useful answer.

**SERA**
I don't know.

**CAEL (V.O.)**
Did we cause it?

**SERA**
I don't know.

**CAEL (V.O.)**
Can we stop it?

Sera cannot manufacture certainty.

**SERA**
I don't know.

A beat.

**CAEL (V.O.)**
Then why tell me?

Sera looks at the personal-message indicator, now marked opened.

**SERA**
Because you asked.

She presses TRANSMIT.

For the first time, a physical relay inside Kestrel engages.

**SERA**
Cael. Listen to me.

The signal fractures.

She gives him the little information the archive contains: infrastructure failures, likely safe depth, time sequence.

Cael repeats it back.

Other voices appear faintly behind him as he moves.

**CAEL (V.O.)**
Sera—

The channel drops.

**SERA**
Cael?

Nothing.

**SERA**
Cael.

Normal static.

She does not call again.

---

## SC08 — WE HEARD YOU

Sera sits alone.

The station sounds return one by one.

She begins an incident report.

**SERA**
Kestrel incident log. Reciprocal contact confirmed on restricted frequency.

She stops.

Deletes the line.

Starts again.

**SERA**
Kestrel incident log. I initiated prohibited transmission on the restricted frequency.

A physical click from the receiver wall.

Sera looks up.

One receiver has activated.

Then another, several meters away.

A third.

Different voices emerge.

Different languages.

Different transmission qualities.

Dozens.

Then hundreds.

The translation system struggles, finding repeating semantic structures.

Sera stands.

She does not touch the controls.

The voices converge imperfectly around one meaning.

**VOICES**
We heard you.

Sera listens.

Not afraid.

Not comforted.

Changed.

**EXT. KESTREL LISTENING STATION — SPACE**

The station is still tiny.

The antenna points into darkness.

Cut to black.

**END**
