# Reference Film Outline

Target runtime: approximately 18 minutes.

## Act / beat shape

### 0:00–2:00 — Hook

Open on an immediately understandable task with one visually striking inconsistency.

### 2:00–6:00 — Mission

Establish protagonist, companion, rules and objective with minimal exposition.

### 6:00–11:00 — Discovery

The apparent problem becomes something larger. Evidence contradicts accepted history or physics.

### 11:00–16:00 — Choice

The characters must act before they fully understand what they found. The choice reveals character and carries a cost.

### 16:00–18:00 — Payoff / aperture

Resolve the immediate danger or mission. Reveal one precise implication that expands the universe without turning the ending into a trailer.

## Production constraint

Prefer tension, atmosphere, strong environments and a small cast over crowd-heavy spectacle. This improves both storytelling focus and generative continuity.
