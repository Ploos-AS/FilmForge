# Universe Bible — Seed

This file intentionally starts with constraints rather than finished lore.

## Creative target

Build an original science-fiction setting that can support multiple films while allowing the first 15–20 minute story to stand alone.

## Required qualities

- instantly legible central premise
- mystery beyond the first film
- distinctive visual language
- technology with rules and consequences
- human-scale emotional stakes
- room for multiple locations and eras
- no dependence on existing franchise lore
- expandable without requiring endless exposition

## First-film constraints

The audience should understand the immediate goal quickly, encounter one compelling anomaly or contradiction, receive a satisfying dramatic payoff, and leave with a larger unanswered question.

## Worldbuilding workflow

Before canon is frozen:

1. Generate multiple universe pitches.
2. Score premise, emotional potential, visual identity, originality, production feasibility and franchise depth.
3. Stress-test the strongest candidates.
4. Combine only compatible strengths.
5. Freeze a compact canon for Film #1.
6. Keep speculative future lore outside canon until needed.
