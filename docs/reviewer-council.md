# Reviewer Council

FilmForge M1.2 uses six independent review roles before canon selection:

- Story editor: dramatic engine and first-film strength.
- Audience editor: immediate curiosity, clarity and memorable promise.
- World editor: rules, expansion capacity and lore debt.
- Visual editor: distinctive imagery and cinematic opportunities.
- Production editor: cast/location burden, continuity and generative feasibility.
- Originality editor: familiar combinations and opportunities for differentiation.

Reviews record 0–100 criterion scores plus strengths, risks and flags.

Supported flags include `fatal`, `derivative-risk`, `production-risk`, `lore-risk`, `continuity-risk`, and `high-variance`.

Arithmetic ranking is evidence, not the creative decision. A polarizing concept with exceptional upside must not automatically lose to a merely safe average concept.
