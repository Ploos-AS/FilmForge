# Universe Foundry

Universe Foundry is the pre-canon stage for FilmForge.

A candidate is not lore. It is a hypothesis that must survive review before any detail becomes canonical.

## Review roles

- **Story editor** — premise clarity, dramatic engine and first-film strength.
- **Audience editor** — immediate curiosity, accessibility and memorable promise.
- **World editor** — expansion capacity, internal rules and avoidance of lore debt.
- **Visual editor** — recognizable imagery and cinematic opportunities.
- **Production editor** — feasibility with current generative video, continuity burden, cast and location count.
- **Originality editor** — detects overly familiar combinations and pushes differentiation.

Each role scores 0–100 with a short rationale. Scores are evidence for discussion, not an automatic creative decision.

## Funnel

1. Seed 10–20 deliberately different concepts.
2. Reject concepts without a compelling 15–20 minute first film.
3. Multi-role review.
4. Shortlist 3–5.
5. Stress-test each with a logline, protagonist, opening image, midpoint discovery, ending and three sequel-scale stories.
6. Produce visual-development prompts for finalists.
7. Human selection.
8. Freeze only Film #1 canon; leave future possibilities explicitly non-canon.

## Rule

Do not average away interesting risk. A polarizing high-concept candidate can be more valuable than a universally adequate one.
