# Review process

Universe candidates are reviewed independently before canon selection.

Review perspectives cover story, audience appeal, worldbuilding, visual identity, production feasibility, and originality.

Each review records numeric criterion scores together with strengths and risks. Rankings are decision support only; the final selection remains an explicit editorial decision.

A concept may advance despite a lower average when it has exceptional creative upside. Production constraints for the first reference film are considered separately from the long-term potential of the universe.
